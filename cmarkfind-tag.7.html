NAME

cmarkfind-tag — give a page your own tags

SYNOPSIS

cmarkfind tag ADDRESS TAG…

cmarkfind tag -list [ADDRESS]

DESCRIPTION

Give a page tags, your own words for what it is about. A tag is 1 to 64 letters, digits, - or _ and is folded to lower case. Tags are not part of the page text: a plain word does not match a tag. Find pages by tag with cmarkfind search -tag TAG; give -tag more than once and a page must carry every tag. Tags are kept by forget and by a refill. Front matter tags are not read.

OPTIONS

-list
Show every tag with the number of pages that carry it, or with an ADDRESS, that page’s tags.
-json
Write the result as JSON.

EXAMPLES

cmarkfind tag https://example.org/oberon.md compilers history
cmarkfind search -tag compilers
cmarkfind search -tag compilers -tag history oberon
