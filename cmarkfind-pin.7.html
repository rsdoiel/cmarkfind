NAME

cmarkfind-pin — keep a page near the top of the results

SYNOPSIS

cmarkfind pin ADDRESS…

cmarkfind pin -list

DESCRIPTION

Pin pages. A pinned page that matches a search is listed before the other matches; among the pinned ones, and among the others, the order is as before. A pin never makes a page match, and a page marked gone is not searched.

ADDRESS is the page’s published address as search shows it, an http:// or https:// address, matched as written. Pinning twice does nothing. An address the corpus does not hold may be pinned (the page may be filled later); that is noted on standard error. Pins are kept by forget and by a refill.

OPTIONS

-list
Show the pinned addresses with their titles, and whether each page is stored, gone or not in the corpus.
-json
Write the result as JSON.

EXAMPLES

cmarkfind pin https://example.org/oberon.md
cmarkfind pin -list
cmarkfind search -pinned compiler
